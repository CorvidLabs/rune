# Requirements

1. The limitation is recorded with the measurement that produced it, not as a
   preference.
2. The claim covers only the probes that actually differ. One that is identical
   either way is labelled baseline, not evidence.
3. The reproduction is runnable.
4. Nothing about the shipped renderer changes.
